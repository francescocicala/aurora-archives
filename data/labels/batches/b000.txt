Probably the finest display Of the aurora 6oreals ever witnessed in this latitude was observed on Sunday night last, between 10 and o'clock. To give description or even much of an idea OF the phenomenon is impossible Perhaps the grandest display occurred between l and z o'clock, at which time the whole heavens were lighted up with the electric fires. From all points in the northern SKY between the east and the west long pencils or paths of light would stream upward from an arch, below which all was dark, to the very zenith and beyond, While occasionally flashes OF light resembling sheet lightning' would spread over the whole. At the terminal points OF the arch and at central point overhead would be seen q concentration Of light resembling an intense fire reflected upon clouds. The southern sky was filled with floating masses OF white, vapor-like clouds, which went whrling away to vanish as they approached the southern horizon.

EVENT 1859-09-26
- Nothing in the articles supports an aurora on 1859-09-26 itself. Every report concerns the great storm of Aug 28-29 and Sept 1-2 1859 (plus Honolulu Sept 4), printed in late Sept and early Oct.
- Supported nights: Aug 28 (into Aug 29), about 10 independent accounts (Dallas, Cincinnati, San Francisco, Sacramento, Baltimore, Havana, Europe); Sept 1 (into Sept 2), about 4 (Boston, New Braunfels TX, California, at sea).
- Distinct places: England, Germany, Brussels, Rome, Havana, Jamaica, Dallas, New Braunfels, Cincinnati, Baltimore, Boston, Harrisburg, California, Honolulu.
- Southernmost: Jamaica, about 18.0 N (undated report; Honolulu 21.3 N is dated).
- Features: red/crimson colour; pyramids near zenith; light bright enough to read print; widespread telegraph disruption; multiple nights; seen in tropics.
- One unsupported item: 1859-09-26_20 jokes about an aurora "last week" but gives no real description.
- Verdict: NOT SUPPORTED for Sept 26; no sightings near that date, only retrospective coverage of the late Aug/early Sept storm.
- Best documents of the real storm: 1859-09-26_09 "impossible to communicate between the telegraph stations"; 1859-09-26_02 "whole northern heavens again on fire"; 1859-09-26_34 "grand illumination of the heavens... fairly beat all".

Night(s): 1860-09-06 (Thursday), about 25 independent reports in file; also a second night 1860-09-15 (NY Herald, Cumberland, Ebensburg; 3 reports), reports for 09-07 (Augusta/Columbus GA; 3 items), and 09-04 (Topeka, 1).
Places: New York, Philadelphia, Cincinnati, Dayton, Evansville, Emporia, Davenport, St Louis, Memphis, Macon MS, Charleston SC, Yorkville SC, Winchester TN, New Orleans, Nevada City and other CA towns, plus a ship at sea. Southernmost: New Orleans, LA (30.0).
Features: red/crimson to blood-red common; rosy field up to the zenith with convergence overhead (NY Tribune); big canopy in the south (Herald); fire alarms rung in New Orleans and Charleston; telegraph disrupted, Boston-New York without batteries; seen on both coasts.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - dozens of independent sightings across the continent on the night of Sept 6, 1860.

Distinct places for Sept 1: New York, Boston/Worcester, Alexandria VA, Staunton, Woodsfield OH, Athens TN, Okolona MS, Lancaster SC, Abbeville SC, Key West, Bermuda, New Orleans/Mobile.

Features: red/crimson (Key West "red as blood", Okolona, Lancaster, Athens); seen in southern sky (New York); telegraph worked without batteries (Boston-Portland, Washington-Pittsburgh); morning display 2-4am Friday; multiple nights.
Reprints (Cincinnati Gazette, NY Times, Journal of Commerce, Boston/Montreal telegrams) counted once.

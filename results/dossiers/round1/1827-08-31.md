- Night supported: 1827-08-28 (Tuesday evening), about 7 independent reports (Salem, Worcester, Portsmouth NH, Providence, Baltimore, Richmond, Wilmington-09); the candidate date 08-31 is off by 3 days.
- Weaker nights: 1827-08-27 (Monday, bright aurora at Richmond), 1827-09-09 (Wilmington, Worcester; 2 reports) and 09-10 (Worcester).
- Distinct places: Salem MA, Worcester MA, Portsmouth NH, Providence RI, Baltimore MD, Richmond VA, Wilmington (state unclear, taken as DE), Harpers Ferry (reprint).
- Southernmost: Richmond, VA (37.5).
- Features: great luminous arch/belt across the zenith drifting south, plus ordinary aurora in north; comet-tail streamers at Baltimore; purple tinge; reddish glow on Sept 9; multiple nights.
- Verdict: CONFIRMED - many independent accounts on the evening of 28 Aug 1827.

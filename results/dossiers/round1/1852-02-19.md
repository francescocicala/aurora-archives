Night supported: 1852-02-19 (Thursday), about 10 independent sighting reports; Philadelphia Ledger says also Wednesday 18th (one account, reprinted 3 times; New Orleans paper also dates it 18th).
Distinct places: Worcester MA, Middlebury VT, Staunton VA, Newport RI, Ebensburg PA, Washington DC, Lewistown PA, New York, Buffalo, Philadelphia.
Southernmost: Staunton, VA (38.2); Washington DC 38.9 close behind.
Features: streamers to zenith, whole sky aflame at Newport, lasting until near daylight; "grandest display for years".
Telegraph disruption: strong currents on lines from Philadelphia/Washington, Boston lines worked without batteries.
Colour: no red reported for 19 Feb; a crimson aurora at Nevada City CA early on 21 Feb is a separate report.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - many independent sightings across the northeast and mid-Atlantic on the night of 19 Feb 1852.
Key IDs:

 BRILLIANT CELESTIAL PHENoMEEoN.-For seve- raj hours, on Monday night. the atmosphere was rendered luminous by the most brilliant Aurora Borealis. The lights took every possible shape, and shot athwart the heavens with the rapdity almost of lightning. At time, about eleven o'clock, the lights arose from every point of the compass, meeting in the centre, and thus forming a superb arch. Suddenly one of the sides changed to deep red color, and Immediately afterwards other all over the armament. This spectacle was unusually brilliant and beautiful, and large num- bers of our citizens were out all night witnessing the phenomenon.-N. Y. Courier.   The same exhibition of the Northern Lights, as has been stated, was witnessed in this city, though probably not of such gorgeous appearance.

Night supported: 1851-09-29 (Monday), about 15 independent reports (reprints of Wilmington Herald/NY Courier counted once). A possible second night 1851-10-09 (Davenport, telegraph disturbed) rests on one report.
Observing places: Martinsburg WV, Washington DC, New York, Raleigh NC, Wilmington NC, Charlotte NC, Warrenton NC, Lynchburg VA, King William Co. VA, Charleston SC, Hartford CT, Springfield MA, Montpelier VT, Racine WI, St. Louis (telegraph).
Southernmost: Charleston, SC (32.8); next Wilmington NC (34.2, via Herald quoted by Raleigh papers).
Features: deep red/crimson glow and streamers; arch/corona toward zenith; seen from Vermont to South Carolina and Wisconsin.
Telegraph disruption: Boston-New York lines inoperable, Hartford, St. Louis, magnet currents reversed.
Light enough to read print at Racine; moon cast no shadow.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - many independent sightings on the evening of 29 Sept 1851 across the eastern US.

 SOUTHERN LIGHTs.-Capt Howes, Of the ship Southern Cross, reports to the Alta of having wit messed splendid auroral display while off Cape Horn   On the night of the ID of September, during a tremendous gale, wonderful phenomena manifest ed ITSELF The spectacle of an Aurora Australis or Southern Lights, was witnessed. 1t commenced about half past one o'clock in the morning, and in creased in splendor till towards daylight, when it gradually faded before the light of day. Our inform states that the whole heavens were of deep red which color was reflected in the ocean, upon which a fearful sea was running. These were surmounted by combs not of the usual white, but almost blood red. Some of the crew much frightened. Once during the night tremendous hail and snow squall hustled upon the ship. Through the whole of this the flames assumed the same roseate hue, and when spray flew over the ship 1t fell to the le ward in ruddy showers.

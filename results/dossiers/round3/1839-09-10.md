Night supported: 1839-09-03 (Tuesday evening), not 09-10; about 7 independent reports (Boston, New York, Edgefield SC, Greenville SC, New Orleans, Bermuda; Boston account reprinted several times).
Places: Boston MA, New York, Edgefield SC, Greenville SC, New Orleans LA, Hamilton (Bermuda).
Southernmost: New Orleans, LA (30.0 N); Bermuda 32.3 N.
Features: purple-red to white veil; streamers from zenith east and west; blazing-sun corona near zenith; light bright enough to read print and like moonlight or rising sun; fire bells rung in New Orleans.
Seen to the far south, unusual for this latitude; no telegraph mention.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - many independent sightings from Boston to New Orleans on the same night, 3 Sept 1839.

EVENT 1853-07-18
Night(s): 1853-07-18 (Belmont OH, Newburgh NY: 2 independent reports); Newport RI report says "Tuesday night" (07-19) but describes same arch, so probably same event (1 more).
Observing places: St Clairsville OH, Newburgh NY, Newport RI. Southernmost: St Clairsville, OH (40.1).
Features: white arch spanning east to west through zenith at about 11pm, stars visible through; columns shooting to zenith; no red; no telegraph note.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - three independent reports of the same unusual arch/aurora within a day, though a weak summer event with a one-day date ambiguity.
Best IDs:
1853-07-18_02 "complete an arch last night at 11 o'clock, as rainbow"
1853-07-18_00 "Northern lights were unusually distinct, and shot up towards the zenith"
1853-07-18_01 "bright light resembling the Aurora Borealis, from which streams arose"

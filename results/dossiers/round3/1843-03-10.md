1843-03-10 evidence
- Night: probably 1843-03-07 (evening before the New Haven notice); 1 sighting report only.
- Observing place: New Haven, CT (41.3 N), also the southernmost.
- Features: faint "twilight form" aurora in the north, moderately bright late; overshadowed by a great comet and zodiacal light.
- Nearly all other articles are about the comet or zodiacal light, which were confused with aurora.
- Night is uncertain by a day; no multiple-night or telegraph evidence.
- Verdict: WEAK, one modest sighting amid comet and zodiacal light coverage.
- Best ID: 1843-03-10_06 "An aurora borealis, of the twilight form, appeared through the evening"

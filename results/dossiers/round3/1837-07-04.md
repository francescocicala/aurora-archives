Night supported: 1837-07-01 (Saturday evening, not 07-04); about 7 independent reports (Baltimore, Norfolk, Maumee OH, Charlotte NC, Cheraw SC, Newport RI, New York), plus reprints.
Places: Baltimore MD, Norfolk VA, Maumee City OH, Charlotte NC, Cheraw SC, Newport RI, New York.
Southernmost: Cheraw, SC (34.7 N); Charlotte NC (35.2) next.
Features: fan of rays white, purple, rose; crimson ray; columns past zenith; flickering; red-tipped columns; seen several hours; faint aurora to the east and south at New York.
Cheraw notes no earlier sighting known so far south; no telegraph mention.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - multiple independent papers from Rhode Island to South Carolina describe the same Saturday night, 1 July 1837.

Only fresh October sightings: San Francisco 3:10 am Oct 18 (night of Oct 17) and Alexandria VA Tuesday night Oct 18.
That is 2 independent reports (Alta item reprinted twice), 5-6 days after the candidate date.
Vague items: Worcester "northern lights nearly every night"; London papers on frequent aurora in early October.
Distinct places for the Oct 17-18 reports: San Francisco (37.8), Alexandria VA (38.8); southernmost San Francisco 37.8.
Features: SF display less brilliant than August; no red, corona or telegraph trouble in these.

Night supported: 1837-01-25 (Wednesday evening); 13 articles, roughly 10 independent reports (many reprints of Boston, NY, Philadelphia, Olmsted accounts).
Places: Newport, Providence RI; Boston MA; New York; Philadelphia and Wilmington; Washington DC; New Haven and Litchfield CT; Montpelier, Rutland, Windsor, Middlebury, Burlington VT; Troy, Albany; Portland ME.
Southernmost: Washington, DC (38.9 N); also Wilmington, DE (39.7).
Features: deep red/crimson/vermilion colour causing fire alarms (Philadelphia, Vermont); corona overhead near Pleiades; arch to zenith and south of it; stars visible through light.
Also: near-full moon yet bright; magnetic needle and barometer disturbance (New Haven); second burst 10-11pm; very cold next morning.
Described as the most splendid ever in the US; no telegraph then.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - many independent sightings across the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic on the same night, 25 January.

1861-03-09: strong aurora on Saturday night 9 March 1861, reported by many papers.
Night supported: 1861-03-09; about 6 independent reports (Oxford MS, Sunbury PA with Wheeling WV a likely reprint, Bloomsburg PA, Richmond VA Whig, Alexandria VA, Richmond Dispatch, possibly the same event).
Distinct places: Oxford MS (34.4), Richmond VA (37.5), Alexandria VA (38.8), Wheeling WV (40.1), Sunbury PA (40.9), Bloomsburg PA (41.0).
Southernmost: Oxford, Mississippi, about 34.4 N.
Features: scarlet/red columns toward the zenith; glow mistaken for a distant fire (Richmond fire bells rung); display lasted to midnight.
Also a NY Herald item noting auroral light on three nights Feb-March (place unstated, dates garbled).
Verdict: CONFIRMED - several independent newspapers across MS, VA, WV and PA describe the same Saturday-night display.

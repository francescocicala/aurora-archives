Night(s) supported: 1864-08-24 about 10 pm. 2 independent sighting reports (Hyde Park VT, St. Cloud MN) plus Portland ME arch called a lunar rainbow (same time, not counted).
Observing places: Hyde Park VT 44.6, St. Cloud MN 45.6, Portland ME 43.7 (arch only), Presque Isle/Fort Fairfield ME 46.7 (undated). Southernmost real sighting: Portland arch 43.7 / Hyde Park 44.6.
Features: E-W belt/arch crossing zenith, moving tints, lasted ~15 min; St. Cloud had aurora in north. No red or telegraph mention.
Maine travel letter (1864-08-24_03) has vivid streamers but is undated and may not be this night.
Verdict: WEAK - only two aurora-labelled reports of an unusual arch on the same night.

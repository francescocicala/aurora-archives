Night(s) supported: 1865-08-02 (Wednesday evening into early 08-03). About 6 independent sighting reports (Indianapolis, Litchfield, Norfolk, New Haven, Newburyport, Hartford; reprints counted once).
Observing places: Indianapolis IN 39.8, Norfolk VA 36.9 (southernmost), Litchfield CT 41.7, New Haven CT 41.3, Newburyport MA 42.8, Hartford CT 41.8.
Features: corona/canopy at zenith, pillars and arch, amethyst glow in north; bright enough to read church clocks; telegraph Boston-Springfield worked on auroral current (08-02/03). No red noted.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - many independent reports from several states on the same night, with telegraph effects.
Best IDs: 1865-08-03_03 "canopy of light, flashing in rapidly succeeding waves ... to the magnetic zenith"; 1865-08-03_06 "aurora was so vivid ... time of night on the church clocks"; 1865-08-03_01 "whole northern hemisphere was in flames".

1846-10-20 evidence
- Night supported: 1846-10-19 (Monday evening); 2 independent reports (Boston, Washington), plus a third Washington notice of uncertain date.
- Separate at-sea sighting on 1846-09-19 (ship near 40.5 N) is outside this event.
- Places: Boston MA, Washington DC, at sea; southernmost land place Washington (38.9 N).
- Features: Boston "unusual lustre", about a quarter of the sky lit; Washington faint, low in north.
- No red colour, corona or telegraph disruption; single night.
- Verdict: WEAK, two or three modest independent sightings, only in the Boston and Washington area.
- Best IDs: 1846-10-20_00 "Northern Lights shone last evening with unusual lustre"
- 1846-10-20_01 "Aurora Borealis discernible on Monday evening ... faint"

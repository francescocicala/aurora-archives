Night supported: 1833-05-17 (Friday), not 05-22; Alexandria paper dates it "evening of the 17th". About 3 independent reports (Alexandria, Charlestown WV, Philadelphia via Martinsburg reprint); Alexandria items are the same paper repeated.
Places: Alexandria VA, Charlestown WV, Philadelphia PA (Martinsburg reprints Philadelphia).
Southernmost: Charlestown, WV (38.4 N) (Alexandria 38.8).
Features: columns shooting to zenith, auroral arch east to west, ribbon band from southeast crossing near zenith, stars seen through columns, thousands in the streets.
Described as one of the most brilliant ever seen; no red or telegraph mention.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - multiple independent papers and places describe the same brilliant Friday night display of 17 May.
Best IDs:

1833-05-22_01 "drew into the streets many thousands of people, with upturned visages"

1839-09-14 evidence
- Night supported: 1839-09-03 (Tuesday evening, election night); about 8 independent reports (Boston Patriot and Transcript counted separately; reprints once).
- Candidate nights 09-14/09-15 themselves have no sightings; articles all concern 09-03.
- Observing places: Boston, New York, Edgefield SC, Greenville SC, New Orleans, Hamilton Bermuda, Burlington VT (7).
- Southernmost: New Orleans, LA, 30.0 N (Hamilton, Bermuda 32.3 N next).
- Features: purple-red to white colours, corona near zenith with rays, light bright enough to read, seen over whole sky.
- Also widespread across US and Bermuda; fire bells rung in New Orleans; steamer Great Western at sea reportedly saw it.
- No telegraph disruption (pre-telegraph era); single night only.
- Verdict: CONFIRMED (night 1839-09-03), since many independent sightings across 7 places on the same night.
- Best IDs: 1839-09-14_03 "rays were of pale reddish hue" ; 1839-09-14_09 "beautifully brilliant at New Orleans ... bells were rung for fire"
- 1839-09-14_08 "Aurora Borealis of the 3d was plainly seen at Hamilton"

 CELESTIAL PHENoMENoN.-The Bes- ton Journal of the 10th, has the following:   The upturned gaze of thousands was di- rected last evening upon an unusual sight in the heavens. A magnificent arch, spanning the sky between the eastern and western horizons, was visible for the space of an hour after s IL o'clock. The arch appeared to the view about six feet in width, and of the consistency of the dens- est portion of the late comets tail. The sky otherwise, with the exception of a faint Aurora in the north, was perfectly cloudless and clear, and the phenomenon made brilliant appearance. Many sur misses were uttered, some regarding it as an omen of peace, while others maintained that it was signal that the East and West were now firmly united, and copper headism defunct.

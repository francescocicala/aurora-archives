EVENT 1859-12-15
Night 1: 1859-12-12 into early morning of 13 Dec: 3 independent reports (Bellows Falls VT, Boston/Cambridge MA, Lockport NY; Lockport reprinted).
Night 2: about 1859-12-14 (into 15 Dec): 3 reports (Independence IA, Muscatine IA, Norfolk VA), weaker than the 12th; dating of Norfolk item inferred.
Places: Bellows Falls, Boston, Lockport, Independence, Muscatine, Norfolk VA; Perth Scotland (undated). Southernmost northern place: Norfolk VA 36.9.
Also Sept 1859 aurora australis off Cape Horn (about -56) and Australia in reprints, not part of the Dec event.
Features: red and crimson colours, brightest morning of 13th lasted until sunrise, light pillar over sun at Cambridge; streamers not reaching zenith; two nights; no telegraph item.
Verdict: CONFIRMED - several independent sightings on 12 Dec and again about 14 Dec across New England, NY, Midwest and Virginia.

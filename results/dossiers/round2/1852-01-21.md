Night supported: 1852-01-19 (Monday), roughly 9-10 independent reports (Worcester, Dubuque, Raleigh, Richmond x2 papers, Washington, Portsmouth OH, New York ferry); candidate 01-21 itself not supported.
Places: Worcester MA, Dubuque IA, Raleigh NC, Richmond VA, Washington DC, Portsmouth OH, New York NY. Southernmost: Raleigh NC, 35.8.
Features: pink/white and blush-red lights; arch with streamers; aurora lasted past midnight; all in extreme cold (record cold); fire alarms in cities; fourth Raleigh aurora since October.
Minor: a Worcester 25 Jan note and a Cecil Whig piece of week-after nights are undated/uncertain.
Verdict: CONFIRMED (for 19 Jan, not 21 Jan) - many independent reports on the same Monday night across the East and Midwest.
Best IDs: 1852-01-21_02 "whole Northern horizon was illuminated with ever-changing pink and white lights"
1852-01-21_05 "patches of blush red and faint streamers"
1852-01-21_03 "Northern Heavens were illuminated by a most splendid Aurora Borealis"

Eapen, P. E., Girish, T. E., Gopkumar, G., Haritha, V. G. (2026). *Investigations on the Extreme Space Weather Conditions During the Years 1841-1877 Using Geomagnetic Observations in Trivandrum, Singapore, Bombay and Madras.* Preprints.org, not peer-reviewed. doi:10.20944/preprints202601.0687.v1. Checked against the PDF on 2026-11-12.

What the PDF says about our storms:

**Table 5** (Trivandrum and Bombay, 1855–1877). GW is the Greenwich class (A = intense, G = great; Maunder 1905). Tvm Dst is the paper's inferred Dst.
- 1860 "6,7 Sep": GW G; Tvm ΔH 476.8 nT; Bombay ΔH 344.8 nT; Tvm Dst 466.5 nT.
- 1860 "6-7 Aug": GW A; Tvm Dst 249.0 nT. There is also 1860 "14-Aug": GW G; Tvm Dst 531.4 nT.
- 1860 "28-Mar": GW A; Tvm Dst 603.5 nT. The text calls it a "super storm".
- 1861 "9-10 Mar": no class; Tvm Dst 198.0 nT.
- 1866 "20,21 Feb": GW G; Tvm Dst 566.9 nT in the table but 556.9 nT in the text. The text says it was "Recorded as a great storm in Greenwich" and occurred near sunspot minimum.

**Table 1** (Madras, 1846–1851):
- 1851 "3-Sep": A; Dst estimate 203.2 nT.
- 1851 "29-Sep": G; Dst estimate 344.5 nT.

**Table 6** (Greenwich great storms 1852–1869, adapted from Eapen's 2009 PhD thesis). GW H is the Greenwich H range; HEL Ak is the Helsinki Ak value the paper uses.
- 1860 Aug 8–11: GW H 450 nT; HEL Ak 92.
- 1860 Aug 12–13: GW H 600 nT; HEL Ak 85.
- 1860 Sep 6–7: GW H 425 nT; HEL Ak 105.
- 1866 Feb 21–22: GW H 400 nT; HEL Ak 108.
- 1852 Feb 18–19 is discussed in the text: Bombay ΔH about 1500 nT, a great storm at Greenwich and in Russia.

What this means for us:
- The August and September 1860, September 1851 and February 1866 storms are known as **magnetic** storms. Greenwich classed them as great storms (Maunder 1905). They appear in Eapen's 2009 PhD thesis and in this 2026 preprint, which gives magnetometer-based intensity estimates.
- Neither source analyses the aurorae (where they were seen, or how far south) or the US telegraph impacts. What Aurora Archives adds is that newspaper evidence and its low-latitude extent.
- Helsinki Ak differs between sources. Eapen's Table 6 gives HEL Ak 105 for September 1860. Our value, 186 for 7 Sep UT, comes from the FMI file `Hels_H_K1844-97_05`. The two likely come from different Ak derivations (Eapen cites Nevanlinna's earlier series), and this should be reconciled before quoting either. Our percentile ranks within one consistent series are unaffected.
